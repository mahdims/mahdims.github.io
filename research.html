Research

Test-time intelligence for frozen models: verifiers, process rewards, memory, and compute allocation, grounded in verifiable signals.

A fixed model can make better decisions at inference time if it has a reliable signal to check itself against. My background in operations research supplies those signals: solvers, feasibility checks, and bounds give exact verification at low cost. The four themes below share that structure. Work under anonymous review is described without titles or venues; papers move to the publications page as decisions and preprints appear.

Process supervision and verifiers for agents

Score an agent's intermediate steps, not only its final answer, so that selection and search can happen before a costly or irreversible action is taken.

  • MASPRM: Multi-Agent System Process Reward Model
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2510.24803 (2025)
  • Evaluating LLM Reasoning in the Operations Research Domain with ORQA
    Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (2025)
  • Submissions under review on checking a generative model's candidate actions before they run. under review
  • An evaluation harness for coding agents that change a production optimization solver, scored on correctness and solver performance. in progress

Memory and adaptation without gradients

Let a frozen model improve across runs by storing and retrieving what worked, instead of fine-tuning it.

  • SEDIMA: memory for search agents
    EMNLP 2026 workshop, accepted as a spotlight
  • Submissions under review on memory for frozen models and on transferable designs for multi-agent systems. under review
  • HERMES, a memory architecture for multi-agent systems. in progress

    LION 20: Learning and Intelligent Optimization Conference (2026)
  • Submissions under review on compute allocation for embodied agents and on representation quality in vision-language models. under review
