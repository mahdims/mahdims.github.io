Principal OR/AI Scientist, Kinaxis

Google Scholar: 574 citations, h-index 10 (Sep 2026)

Test-time intelligence for frozen models: verifiers, process rewards, memory, and compute allocation, grounded in verifiable signals.

I work on test-time intelligence for frozen models: process rewards and verifiers for agents, non-parametric memory, and policies that decide how much compute a decision deserves, with optimization solvers as a source of exact, cheap verification. I have shipped a production routing engine and OptvEvolve, an LLM-driven framework that evolves and tunes optimization algorithms, and at Kinaxis I lead AI acceleration for the production MILP solver and the evaluation infrastructure for coding agents that work on it. My work has appeared at NeurIPS, AAAI, ACL, INFOR, EJOR, and IISE Transactions, and I am lead inventor on multiple filed patents, four of them published.

Before Kinaxis I was Senior Staff Researcher and AI Lead of the OptVerse solver at Huawei Vancouver (2022 to 2026), a Postdoctoral Fellow at Polytechnique Montréal / CIRRELT with Prof. Michel Gendreau, and earned my Ph.D. in operations research from Koç University.

Research themes

  • Process supervision and verifiers for agents
  • Memory and adaptation without gradients
  • Test-time compute and search
